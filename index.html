Mission 1

🎬 Watch & Discover

Your mission:
Watch the conversation carefully. While watching, try to find:
  • How does the speaker greet their friend?
  • What question do they ask?
  • How does the other person respond?
You don't need to understand every word. Just listen and catch the expressions!
💡 Tip: Listen for short, friendly expressions used between people who know each other.

Mission 4

🎤 Solo Speaking Quest

Follow the steps like a speaking practice app: Listen → Think → Speak → Record → Listen back.

STEP 1 · READ THE SITUATION
Challenge 1
Your friend says:
“Hey! How's it going?”
Your task: Give a natural response.
STEP 2 · GET READY

Think of your response. You can use one of these:

I'm good! Pretty good! Great! How about you?
STEP 3 · SPEAK
🎙️ Tap START when you're ready.
Possible responses: “Pretty good!” or “I'm good! How about you?”
CHALLENGE 2 · NO WORD BANK
Situation:
You meet your friend after school.

Your task: Greet your friend and ask how they are doing.

🎤 Say the whole sentence by yourself. Don't read a full script.
SPEAKING TARGET

Try to include one casual greeting + one question.

Example target: “Hey! How are you doing?”
🎙️ Tap START when you're ready.
🎯 Speaking checklist
Before moving on, check:
  • Did I use a casual greeting?
  • Did I ask a natural question?
  • Was my pronunciation understandable?
