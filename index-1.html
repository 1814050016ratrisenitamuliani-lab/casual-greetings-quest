🧭 Quest Guide

1. Watch
Watch only the conversation clip. Listen for casual greetings and how the speakers respond.
2. Notice
Look for expressions such as Hi, Hey, What's up?, How's it going?, How are you doing?, What's new?
3. Speak
Read the target sentence, tap the microphone, say it naturally, then stop. The game transcribes your speech and gives an approximate pronunciation score.
4. Buddy Talk
Work with a partner. Choose roles A and B, then perform the dialogue aloud.
5. Unlock
Earn XP and badges by completing missions.
